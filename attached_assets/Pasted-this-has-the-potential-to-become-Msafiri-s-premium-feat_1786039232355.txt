this has the potential to become Msafiri's premium feature. Not because it replaces insurance, but because it solves one of the most stressful moments a driver experiences: "I've just had an accident. What do I do now?"

Most people are shaken up after a collision. They forget to take photos, don't remember the exact location, and struggle to explain what happened later. Msafiri can quietly collect that information as it happens.
