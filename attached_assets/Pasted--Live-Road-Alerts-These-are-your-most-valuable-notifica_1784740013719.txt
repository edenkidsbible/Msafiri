Best Practices

Keep notifications:

Relevant to the user's current location, route, or driving habits.
Short (ideally under 60–80 characters for the title).
Timely, especially while the user is driving.
Configurable so users can opt in or out of categories like speed cameras, traffic, weather, police checkpoints, or promotions.
Limited in frequency to avoid notification fatigue. High-priority safety alerts can be immediate, while summaries and engagement messages should be much less frequent.

A combination of real-time road intelligence, personalized commute insights, community recognition, and weekly driving summaries tends to keep navigation app users engaged because the notifications consistently deliver practical value rather than generic reminders to open the app.
