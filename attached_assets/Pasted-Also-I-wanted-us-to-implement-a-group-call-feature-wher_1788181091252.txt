Yes, this is feasible, but I would not launch it as an open “group call” in the WhatsApp/Clubhouse sense.

For Msafiri, the safer and stronger product concept is a Road Channel: a location-based, audio-first room tied to a specific highway or major road, with strict moderation, hands-free design, and automatic joining/leaving prompts based on where the driver is.

Apple and Google both allow user-generated/social communication features, but they expect strong safeguards. Apple requires filtering/reporting/blocking mechanisms and is particularly cautious about random or anonymous chat experiences; Google similarly requires terms of use, reporting, blocking and ongoing moderation for UGC features.

Noise from cars.
