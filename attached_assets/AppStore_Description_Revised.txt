Msafiri: Your Driving Companion

Drive smarter and stay safer on Kenya's roads. Msafiri is a driving and road safety companion built for Kenyan drivers — bringing together speed camera alerts, community road reports, a built-in dashcam, accident assistance, trip tracking and more.

A subscription is required to access all features, available as an in-app purchase.

SPEED CAMERA ALERTS
Stay aware of speed cameras and changing speed limits as you drive. Msafiri alerts you as you approach known camera locations across Kenya, helping you stay within the applicable speed limit.

BUILT-IN DASHCAM
Turn your phone into a dashcam. Record your journeys, capture important moments and keep footage ready whenever you need it.

ACCIDENT ASSISTANT
If you are involved in an incident, Msafiri helps you gather the details that matter — location, photos, dashcam footage and trip information — and organises everything into a clear accident report you can share with your insurer.

ROAD AND COMMUNITY ALERTS
See what other drivers are reporting in real time. View and submit reports for accidents, police checkpoints, Alcoblow locations, traffic, roadworks, potholes, flooding, roadblocks, debris and other road hazards.

ALCOBLOW ALERTS
Receive alerts about reported Alcoblow checkpoints along your route so you are always informed.

TRIP TRACKING
Keep a log of every journey. Review your distance, duration, route and driving statistics, and build a clearer picture of how you drive over time.

DRIVING SCORE
Receive a driving score based on your speed, braking, acceleration and other driving patterns. Use it to understand your habits and work towards a smoother, safer driving style.

VEHICLE SERVICE AND MAINTENANCE
Stay on top of your vehicle's service history. Log completed services and use your estimated odometer to track when maintenance is next due. Share a vehicle with other drivers so their trips count towards the total mileage.

TRIP SHARING
Let family or friends follow your journey live — useful for anyone who wants to know you have arrived safely.

FIND NEARBY AND NAVIGATION
Discover petrol stations, restaurants, shops, gyms and other useful places near you. Explore roads and alerts on Msafiri's map, or use it alongside your preferred navigation app for dedicated safety alerts while you navigate.

Whether you are commuting across Nairobi, heading upcountry or setting off on a long road trip, Msafiri gives you the tools to drive with more confidence and awareness.
