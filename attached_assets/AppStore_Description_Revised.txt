Msafiri: Your Driving Companion

Drive smarter and stay informed on Kenya's roads with Msafiri — a driving and road safety app built for Kenyan drivers.

All features require an active subscription, available as an in-app purchase.

Speed Camera Alerts
Stay aware of speed cameras and changing speed zones while you drive. Get timely alerts as you approach known camera locations and help keep your speed within the applicable limit.

Built-In Dashcam
Turn your smartphone into a dashcam and record your journeys. Save important moments and keep footage available when you need it.

Accident Assistant
If something goes wrong, Msafiri helps you capture incident details — location, photos, dashcam footage and trip information — and organise them into a structured accident report you can share with your insurer.

Road and Community Alerts
Stay informed about what other drivers are seeing. Report and view road incidents including accidents, police checkpoints, Alcoblow locations, traffic, roadworks, potholes, flooding, roadblocks, debris and other hazards.

Alcoblow Alerts
Get alerts about reported Alcoblow locations and stay aware of alcohol testing checkpoints along your journey.

Trip Tracking and Summaries
Keep a record of your journeys with distance, duration, route and driving statistics. Review previous trips and understand more about how you drive.

Driving Score
Get a score based on your driving activity — speed, braking, acceleration and other patterns — and work toward smoother, safer driving.

Vehicle Service and Maintenance
Track your vehicle's service history and use your estimated odometer to know when maintenance may be due. Share a vehicle with other drivers so their trips contribute to the mileage.

Trip Sharing
Share your live journey with family or friends so they can follow your trip in real time.

Find Nearby and Navigation
Discover petrol stations, restaurants, shops, gyms and other useful places nearby. Use Msafiri's map to explore roads and view alerts, or run it alongside your preferred navigation app for dedicated safety alerts while navigating.

Whether you're commuting across Nairobi, travelling upcountry or on a long road trip, Msafiri brings together the tools you need for a more informed drive.
