Msafiri: Your Driving Companion

Drive smarter and stay informed on Kenya’s roads with Msafiri, a driving and road safety app built for Kenyan drivers.

Get speed camera alerts, road alerts, a built-in dashcam, accident assistance, vehicle service reminders, trip tracking and community-powered reports — all in one driving companion.

Speed Camera Alerts

Stay aware of speed cameras and changing speed zones while you drive. Get timely alerts as you approach known speed camera locations and help keep your driving within the applicable speed limit.

Msafiri is continually expanding its speed camera coverage across Kenya.

Built-In Dashcam

Turn your smartphone into a dashcam and record your journeys while you drive.

Record your road trips, save important moments and keep valuable footage available when you need it. Msafiri can connect your driving activity with your trip history and safety features.

Accident Assistant

If something goes wrong, Msafiri can help you capture important information about the incident.

Accident assistance can help organize details such as your location, trip information, photos, dashcam footage and other evidence into an accident report that you can save and share with your insurer or other relevant parties.

Insurance-Ready Accident Reports

After an accident, collecting information can be stressful. Msafiri helps organize your accident evidence into a structured report.

Keep your incident details, location, photos, video evidence, driver information and notes together so you can provide relevant information when making an insurance claim.

Community reports help drivers stay aware of changing road conditions.

Alcoblow Alerts

Get alerts about reported Alcoblow locations and stay aware of alcohol testing checkpoints along your journey.

Trip Tracking and Summaries

Keep a record of your journeys and see useful trip information such as distance, duration, route and driving statistics.

Review your previous trips and understand more about how you drive.

Driving Score

Get a driving score based on your driving activity, including factors such as speed, braking, acceleration and other driving patterns.

Use your score to understand your driving habits and work toward smoother, safer driving.

Vehicle Service and Maintenance

Keep your vehicle's maintenance history organized.

Add your vehicle, record when services were completed and use your estimated odometer to help track when maintenance may be due.

You can also share a vehicle with other drivers so their trips contribute to the vehicle's mileage, helping keep service tracking more accurate.

Trip Sharing

Share your journey with people you trust and let them follow your trip.

Useful for family members, friends and anyone who wants to know where you are while you're on the road.

Navigation and Maps

Use Msafiri's map experience to explore roads, view nearby alerts and find useful places around you.

You can also use Msafiri alongside your preferred navigation app when you want dedicated road and safety alerts while navigating.

Drive With More Awareness

Whether you're commuting across Nairobi, travelling upcountry or taking a long-distance road trip, Msafiri brings together the tools you need for a more informed driving experience.

Msafiri is built for drivers who want more than a map.
